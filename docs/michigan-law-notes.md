### 2026 EPIC cost-of-living amounts (Treasury notice, Jan. 30, 2026)
- Intestate spouse share base: **$301,000** (replaces $150,000) / **$201,000** (replaces $100,000) — MCL 700.2102
- Homestead allowance $30,000 (2402); exempt property $20,000 (2404); family allowance $36,000 (2405)
- Small-estate sworn statement (no real property, net ≤): **$53,000** (3983); 3982(1)-(2) $53,000; 3982(5) $264,000
- Federal estate/gift exemption 2026: **$15,000,000** per person (OBBBA, per ICLE §29.14)

### Intestacy (used by the "what if I do nothing?" calculator)
- 700.2102 spouse: (a) all if no descendant or parent; (c) $301k + ¾ balance if no descendant but a parent; (b),(d),(e) $301k + ½ if all descendants are shared (whether or not spouse has others) or some descendants are not the spouse's; **$201k + ½** if none of decedent's descendants are spouse's.
- 700.2103: descendants by representation → parents → parents' descendants → grandparents/descendants split paternal/maternal → state (2105).
- Stepchildren are not heirs unless adopted (ICLE §7.3; 700.2114).

## 2. Financial power of attorney — Uniform Power of Attorney Act, 2023 PA 187 (eff. July 1, 2024)

- EPIC's old durable POA sections (700.5501–.5505) are **repealed**.
- Signed by principal (or at direction in presence). **Durable** if (a) acknowledged before a notary, or (b) signed before 2 witnesses (neither an agent/successor; one may be the notary). MCL 556.205.
- Durable by default when so executed (556.204). Effective immediately unless springing (556.209); incapacity determined by person named or, absent that, a physician/licensed psychologist in writing.
- **Notarize**: only acknowledged powers get the presumption of genuineness and the third-party acceptance/7-business-day rules (556.205(4), .219, .220). Site always instructs notary + 2 witnesses.
- "Hot powers" need express grant (556.301(1)): trusts, gifts, survivorship, beneficiary designations, delegation, J&S annuity waiver, fiduciary powers, e-communications content, foreign accounts. Non-family agent can't benefit self even with gift power unless expressly allowed (556.301(2)).
- Gifts limited by 556.317 unless the power says otherwise.
- **Agent must sign the Agent's Acknowledgment (556.402) before acting** under a durable power (556.213(2)) — generated for each agent.
- Divorce/separation filing terminates spouse-agent's authority unless power says otherwise (556.210(2)(c)).
- Principal may nominate conservator/guardian; court must appoint most recent nominee (556.208).
- Template: the **Michigan statutory form (556.401)**, used verbatim so banks recognize it, with the client's selections pre-marked and Special Instructions filled from the questionnaire.

## 3. Patient advocate designation (MCL 700.5506–.5515)

- Patient 18+, sound mind; advocate 18+ individual; successors allowed (5507(3)).
- Writing, signed, dated, voluntary; **2 witnesses** who are NOT: spouse, parent, child, grandchild, sibling, presumptive heir, known devisee, physician, patient advocate, or employee of patient's life/health insurer, treating health facility, home for the aged where patient lives, or CMH program/hospital serving patient (5506(4)).
- Must state authority is exercisable only when patient is unable to participate in medical/mental-health decisions; anatomical gift authority survives death (5506(3)).
- Must be put in medical record before implementation.
- Advocate must receive a copy and **sign an acceptance containing substantially the 10 statutory statements** before acting (5507(4)-(5)) — generated for each advocate.
- Inability determined by attending physician + another physician or licensed psychologist in writing (5508); mental-health authority needs physician + mental health practitioner certification (5515(2)).
- Withhold/withdraw life-sustaining treatment only if patient expressed in a **clear and convincing manner** authority and acknowledgment that death could result (5509(1)(e)) — separate initialed paragraph.
- No withholding from a pregnant patient that would result in death (5509(1)(d), 5512(1)).
- Forced medication / non-voluntary inpatient mental health only with clear and convincing authorization (5509(1)(h)); default **off**.
- Revocation by any communication; divorce revokes spouse-advocate unless successor named (5510).
- Remote (audio-visual) witnessing under 700.1202 **expired July 1, 2021** — all signing is in person.
- HIPAA: separate authorization recommended so a named person can get records to establish inability (ICLE §3.40; 45 CFR 164.508).

## 4. Funeral representative designation (MCL 700.3206(2))
- Declarant 18+, sound mind; representative 18+, sound mind. Writing, dated, signed voluntarily.
- Executed EITHER with 2 witnesses (not the representative and not funeral home/health/cemetery/crematory people listed in (2)(c)) OR acknowledged before a notary.
- Funeral home, cemetery, crematory, treating health-care staff can't serve unless spouse/relative.
- Designated representative is #2 in priority after a military designee (3206(3)).

## 5. Ethics and business-model constraints (MRPC; SBM opinions)
- SBM Ethics Opinion **R-25**: for-profit platforms that take a cut of attorney fees / act as referral services violate MRPC 6.3(b), 5.4 fee-sharing and 7.2(c), and risk aiding UPL (5.5(a)). → The site is built to be **operated by the law firm itself**, fees paid to the firm, no revenue split with a nonlawyer.
- Limited-scope representation (MRPC 1.2(b)) with written informed consent → engagement terms accepted at checkout; attorney runs conflict check before accepting.
- Client funds: flat fees — attorney must decide earned-on-receipt vs. trust deposit under MRPC 1.5/1.15; site supports refunds on decline.
- Lawyer must keep independent judgment: nothing is delivered to the client until the attorney approves.

## 7. Will deposit and courtesy packets (extra services)
- A will in a sealed wrapper endorsed with the testator's name, residence, Social Security or Michigan driver's license number (if any), the delivery date, and the deliverer's name may be deposited by the testator **or a person for the testator** with the probate court of the county where the testator lives; the court keeps it and issues a certificate of deposit (MCL 700.2515(1)).
- During life the court releases it only to the testator or someone the testator authorizes in writing (700.2515(2)); after death it is opened and forwarded to the court with jurisdiction on request (700.2515(3)).
- Court fee: **$25** to deposit a will for safekeeping (MCL 600.880c(2)). Courts set their own envelope and release procedures (Kalamazoo County, for example, asks for a 4-1/8 × 9-1/2 envelope with name, address, last four of SSN, driver's license number, and date; release by mail uses its authorization form and a $10 return fee).
- A custodian of a will must deliver it to the court promptly after death (MCL 700.2516).
- Courtesy letters to nonclients state that the firm represents only the client and cannot advise the recipient (MRPC 4.3), and share only what the client authorized (MRPC 1.6(a)).
